How we label evidence

Not every finding is equally strong. We'll tell you which is which.

Nutrition research ranges from firm clinical guidance to early ideas. Content on Nurtre is labeled so you know how much weight each statement deserves.

  • Established guidance

    Reflected in major clinical practice guidelines and supported by consistent evidence.

  • Research finding

    Seen in controlled studies, but not yet strong or consistent enough to be standard guidance.

  • Association

    A link observed in population studies. It doesn't show that one thing causes the other.

  • Emerging hypothesis

    An early idea based on limited or preliminary research. Worth watching, not acting on.
